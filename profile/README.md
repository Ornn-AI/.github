Ornn builds financial products, market data, and software for AI compute. Our
work focuses on benchmark pricing, compute risk management, and the systems that
help buyers, providers, and financial partners understand GPU infrastructure as
a tradable market.

## What We Build

- Compute price indices and market data for GPU infrastructure
- Risk and settlement tooling for AI compute exposure
- Benchmarking systems for GPU quality, performance, and contract standards
- Internal platforms for operating compute, data, and exchange workflows

Most Ornn production systems are private. Public repositories in this
organization are curated releases, datasets, and tooling intended for partners,
researchers, and infrastructure operators.
